HOW TO PLAY

  • Toss: Win the toss to choose whether to Bat or Bowl first.
  • Match Setup: 2 Innings total. Each team gets 2 Wickets.
  • Scoring: Both player and computer select a number from 1 to 6.
  • Different Numbers: If numbers don't match, the batsman scores their selected number of runs.
  • Same Numbers (Match): If both numbers are identical, the batsman is OUT!
  • Target Chase: In 2nd Innings, the chasing side must surpass the 1st Innings score to win.
